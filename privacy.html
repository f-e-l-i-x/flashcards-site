Privacy Policy

Flashcards for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 1 November 2026

Flashcards does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

Your decks, cards, learning progress and settings are saved only on your device. They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups switched on, as for any app; that backup is handled by Apple, not by us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

When you scan a vocabulary list, the app uses the camera, or the photos you choose, to read the words. The text is recognised on your device. Photos are kept in memory only while the scan screen is open and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app sees only the photos you pick, not your photo library.

Speech

Words are read aloud by the voices built into iOS, on your device.

Files you import

When you import a deck file, its cards are added to the app. The file is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
