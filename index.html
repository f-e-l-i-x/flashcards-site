Flashcards

Flashcards turns the vocabulary lists from your textbook into flashcards. Scan a page, then learn the words with spaced repetition, six games, a daily goal and statistics. For iPhone and iPad, in English and German.

Help

Scanning a list. Tap + in the deck list, then Scan Vocab List. Take a photo of the page or choose photos, drag the frame around the word list and tap Read. Check the cards, untick lines you don't want, and tap Add Cards.

Which cards come up? With spaced repetition on (Settings), a session asks the cards that are due, up to 20. With nothing due, "Practise All Cards" goes through the whole deck without changing when cards come up next.

Some decks are missing. Decks for a language you don't learn are hidden, never deleted. Tick the language in Settings to see them again.

Hearing words. The speaker button uses the voices installed on your device. You can add better voices in the iOS Settings app under Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Your data. Decks and progress are kept only on your device and in your device's backup. Deleting the app deletes them.
